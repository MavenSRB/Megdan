## Current implementation

The project has completed roadmap milestones through **6.6a**. The configured vertical slice uses a pure match application service, typed action handlers, authoritative projections, deterministic replay/Undo, map-bound snapshots, and passive Unity presentation; schema v11 persists current continuations, including effective hero/minion Minion-Battle identities, a paused effect frame, a paused after-action displaced-minion correction, and placed token and attached marker components. Tokens expose catalog-owned persistent properties as continuous sources, survive independently of their source hero, clear before a validated Spawn uses their covered Spawn point, and can be removed by the general Clear replacement for an Attack. Active effects and token properties now declare typed value modifiers, restrictions, permissions, immunity, and `Counts As`, which the value, offer, targeting, presence, effective-type, and obstacle queries consult explicitly; every Active-effect creator mints per-activation instance identity through one shared contract, and marker attachments mint theirs through its sibling, so a replayed card creates a fresh effect and a fresh attachment while re-execution inside one resolution stays a no-op. Terrain is a passable obstacle distinct from off-board: a Move-Through mover crosses the official map's 82 declared terrain cells at ordinary cost while no unit may end a Movement there and nothing may be placed there. One shared immunity decision now combines declared activity immunity with standing Heavy immunity for candidate derivation and final mutation. Minion Battle counts effective minions; a removed counts-as hero leaves the board without Defeat/reward/Life consequences, disappears from the configured board, and receives the optional next-action Hero Respawn or resolve-without-action choice; a returned occupancy reactivates and repositions the retained hero view. Both displacements read one statement of how much of a declared distance is optional, so `up to X` admits no distance at all - a direct Move onto the mover's own space, or a zero-space push - as an applied result rather than a decline. A `Choose` offers only the options the acting player can perform: every capability declares what it would report without performing anything, an option proven to apply nothing is projected greyed and carries no invocation, and a mandatory node whose every option is refused pauses with all of them greyed and offers End Turn as the one legal command, which only the player takes. Board objects also Swap, Replace, and Remove through a fifth board port none of them could reach through the displacement commit: a Swap exchanges two occupied spaces in one transaction validated against one pre-effect state, a Replace substitutes a different object in a space that is never empty between them under a declaration stating its source, destination, property transfer and observers, and a Remove takes a piece to its declared supply through a named stage reporting removed or removal-prevented, awarding nothing and admitting no hero. Each of the Swap and the Remove declares which kind of piece it may take, so a card whose text says unit or token takes no other, and one of them is declared a second time with the acting hero as its first object, which is how card text naming your own hero reads. One shared removal stage now governs whether a minion leaves the board, asked by that Remove and by p.13's Defeat alike, so a minion whose removal is prevented keeps its recorded Defeat and its collected coins whichever operation attempted it; only a completed removal asks whether the last effective Battle-Zone minion of a side has gone, and end-of-round Lane-Push requests and both mid-turn causes are recorded but not yet consumed. A target selection declares the node it selects for and offers only the objects that step can act on, judged through the same question the option walk asks and re-read when the answer arrives rather than trusted from the offer. The exact subject-oriented inventory of implemented behavior, configured setup, compatibility boundaries, and remaining gaps lives in [Project/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md](Project/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md) and is maintained by subject rather than milestone chronology.

What the milestone owns is the three subjects rather than the operations: p.19 lets a Swap exchange any two objects and a Remove take any game piece, this card says unit and token, and each branch declares its own narrowing on the operation that performs it, so milestone 6.8l turns the declaration into the offer and the Swap never names the adjacent Hazard while the Remove never names the adjacent minion. - The private target remains an official-setup 3v3 pitch match with Wasp plus five later approved, source-verified heroes, retaining director mode and adding invite-only six-human internet validation before production presentation. Accounts, public matchmaking, public distribution, and production live-service scope remain excluded.
- Declared target platforms are Windows, macOS, and iOS as of 2026-08-13. Windows remains the active reference build target; macOS and iOS are declared, not supported, and iOS player work is unscheduled phase-scale work. See [Project/PRODUCT.md](Project/PRODUCT.md).
- Rules follow the second-printing rulebook, verified card data, and official rulings under [RULES_LEDGER.md](RULES_LEDGER.md). No rule may be invented, simplified, rebalanced, or silently reinterpreted.

- The public source repository and rulebook are authorized under the user's 2026-08-13 permission confirmation. The extracted card corpus, map references, builds, and untracked material remain private; do not add or distribute them without separate explicit authorization and applicable rights.
