## Current implementation

The project has completed roadmap milestones through **6.5a**. The configured vertical slice uses a pure match application service, typed action handlers, authoritative projections, deterministic replay/Undo, map-bound snapshots, and passive Unity presentation; schema v10 persists current continuations, a paused effect frame, a paused after-action displaced-minion correction, and placed token and attached marker components. Tokens expose catalog-owned persistent properties as continuous sources, survive independently of their source hero, clear before a validated Spawn uses their covered Spawn point, and can be removed by the general Clear replacement for an Attack. Active effects and token properties now declare typed value modifiers, restrictions, permissions, immunity, and `Counts As`, which the value, offer, targeting, presence, effective-type, and obstacle queries consult explicitly; every Active-effect creator mints per-activation instance identity through one shared contract, and marker attachments mint theirs through its sibling, so a replayed card creates a fresh effect and a fresh attachment while re-execution inside one resolution stays a no-op. Terrain is a passable obstacle distinct from off-board: a Move-Through mover crosses the official map's 82 declared terrain cells at ordinary cost while no unit may end a Movement there and nothing may be placed there. Every rules-consuming seam now takes the live modification set as a required parameter, and every rule-affecting parameter of the modification query surface is required with it - `RuleModificationSet.Empty`, `TieBreakerState.Unrevealed`, and `RuleObject.None` are the named absent values - while a reflection conformance rule keeps optional defaults out. Every implemented rule naming a unit type declares its intrinsic-or-effective question in the unit-type question ledger, and the p.13 Heavy-immunity accompaniment is the effective type's first production consumer: a hero counting as a minion in the starting Battle Zone protects its Heavy from being targeted. It includes reload-safe Hero/minion combat and the end-of-round Minion Battle with serializable exact-count, one-at-a-time Heavy-last removal choices, now resolved by a configured player-facing prompt with board cues that forwards only the projected typed invocations; Lane-Push requests are recorded but not yet consumed. The exact subject-oriented inventory of implemented behavior, configured setup, compatibility boundaries, and remaining gaps lives in [Project/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md](Project/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md) and is maintained by subject rather than milestone chronology.
