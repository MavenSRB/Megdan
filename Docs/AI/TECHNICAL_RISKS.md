## Temporary-structure register

Every knowingly temporary structure present in `Assets/` is registered here with the milestone that owns its retirement. This register exists because the legacy controller drill survived for the project's entire history without any roadmap milestone scheduling its removal; a structure that no document admits is temporary never becomes anyone's job. The living-document validator warns when a matching structure exists in the source with no row here.
