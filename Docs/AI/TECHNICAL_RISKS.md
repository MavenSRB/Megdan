| `RISK-SCALE-001` | A rules step the source performs simultaneously is serialized by the intra-stage continuation contract, which permits exactly one paused step at a time. Rulebook p.15 has every leveling player upgrade their cards "simultaneously"; milestone 7.5 runs that as one transition taking heroes in hero-id order, one question at a time. The rules outcome is identical and the serialization is faithful - a hero's cards, coins and Level are entirely its own, so no purchase can change what another hero is offered - but at 3v3 up to five players wait while one answers, potentially through several consecutive levels. The same shape will apply to any later simultaneous step routed through the same contract. Two costs ride alongside it: the payload and the per-round derivation both scale with hero count. | Structural rather than speculative, and demonstrated only at a scale where it costs nothing. The mechanism is `SystemTransitionRejectionReason.TransitionAlreadyRunning` plus `HeroLevelUpStepRules.ResolvePurchaseAndUpgrade`, which orders heroes by ID and finishes an outstanding purchase before beginning another - an ordering that is not incidental, because it is also what lets a restored continuation re-derive its pause from the cards alone. At the staged 1v1 scale the exposure is invisible: assist coins reach no friendly hero, so heroes almost never level and the stage usually completes in one command without asking anything. At 3v3 assist coins reach two teammates per hero Defeat, so a hero earns from its teammates' Defeats as well as its own and the stage does real work most rounds. The two costs are arithmetic on measured unit costs rather than estimates, and neither has been measured at 3v3: the payload carries 108 card instances and 108 definitions instead of 36 and 36, which is ~16.6 KB decoded against today's 6,633 B, computed from the 93 B/instance measured at 7.5 and the ~42 B/definition measured at 7.3, and it is serialized once per accepted command and twice per refused one; and one `ResolveStep` call costs about O(heroes x instances x definitions), roughly 70k comparisons at 3v3, which is single-digit milliseconds for a realistic round and bounded by one stage. Medium impact, high confidence in the mechanism and no confidence in the magnitude, because nothing has run at this scale. | Any milestone that raises the hero count above one per team, or that routes a second rules step the source performs simultaneously through the same continuation contract. | 8.25 | Not reassessable before 3v3 scale exists: milestone 7.9's complete match is still the staged 1v1 slice, so it establishes the 36-card reference point this risk is later compared against without measuring anything about it.
