### Exact printed clauses

- **Mind Grip:** "**Choose one —** Perform an action on the card in the previous turn slot of an enemy hero in range; if you would place any tokens this way, place Illusion tokens instead; do not use markers. [Or:] Defeat a minion adjacent to you."
- **Diabolical Laughter:** "Laugh diabolically; if you do, choose up to three times — Swap with an Illusion token in radius. [Or:] Place an Illusion token in an adjacent space. [Or:] Swap two resolved cards of an enemy hero in radius, without canceling active effects."
- **Phantasmal Sentry:** "**Choose one —** Target a hero in range adjacent to an Illusion token in range. [Or:] Target a unit adjacent to you."
- **Phantasmal Warrior / Phantasmal Champion:** "**Choose one —** Before the attack: Move an Illusion token in range up to 1 [2] space[s] to a space adjacent to an enemy hero in range; target that hero. [Or:] Target a unit adjacent to you."
- **Twist Fate:** "Target a unit in range. After the attack: You may swap an enemy unit in range with an Illusion token adjacent to you."
- **Devious Scheme:** "Target a unit in range. After the attack: You may swap an enemy unit in range with an Illusion token in range."
- **Imbue Doubt / Time to Reconsider:** "Choose a color. **Next turn, after playing cards:** An enemy hero in radius discards a card of that color, if able."
- **Crack in Reality:** "Split the board into two sides with a straight line of spaces drawn through your space. **This turn:** Units on either side of the line cannot interact with objects and spaces on the other side of the line as if they did not exist. *(This includes movement, placement, radius-based effects, etc.)*"
- **An Illusion of Choice:** "Choose a color. **Next turn, after playing cards:** Up to two enemy heroes in radius discard a card of that color, if able."
- **Shift Reality:** "Split the board into two sides with a straight line of spaces drawn through your space. **This turn:** Units on either side of the line cannot interact with you, and with objects and spaces on the other side of the line, as if they did not exist."
- **Fleeting Image:** "Place an Illusion token in radius. You may swap with an Illusion token in play."
- **Multiple Projections / Master of Illusions:** "Place up to 2 [3] Illusion tokens in radius. You may swap with an Illusion token in play."
- **Illusionary Force / Illusionary Army:** "Place up to 2 [3] Illusion tokens in radius. **This round:** While you are performing actions, all Illusion tokens count as both tokens and friendly melee minions."
- **What the Hell Are You?:** "Each time you laugh diabolically as part of performing an action, all enemy heroes in radius discard a card, or are defeated."
- **Mind Probe (boxed Handicap):** "Perform an action on the card in the previous turn slot of an enemy hero in range; if you would place any tokens this way, place Illusion tokens instead; do not use markers."

## Settled questions, user 2026-10-04

The user saw the relevant fronts and confirmed all proposed readings. **NB-Q1:** Laughing diabolically is a real optional player choice; each choice triggers the Ultimate once, including when the action was repeated or borrowed. **NB-Q2:** Crack in Reality and Shift Reality choose any of the three straight-line axes through NebKher's space; line spaces belong to neither side, and the chosen line stays fixed for the turn even if he moves. **NB-Q3:** Mind Grip can choose the primary or a printed secondary action on the chosen enemy hero's previous-slot card and perform it with NebKher as the actor. **NB-Q4:** Illusions count as friendly melee minions for action targeting and modifiers only while NebKher performs an action; they do not count at the end-of-round Minion Battle. The user also confirmed three Illusion tokens.

The [Gydion shard](HERO_GYDION.md#source-and-rule-authority) records a direct designer answer: NebKher may use Gydion's card to cast from the spellbook, choosing a spell by name without first looking at it. This applies under NB-Q3 and the project's existing spellbook privacy contract. It does not grant general access to another hero's private cards.

Each is generic typed authority rather than a branch on NebKher or a card name.
