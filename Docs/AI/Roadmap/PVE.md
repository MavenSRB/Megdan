# PvE delivery plan

Return to [workflow](../ROADMAP.md). Decisions live in [PvE design](../Project/PVE_DESIGN.md); authorization lives in the [brief](../SESSION_BRIEF.md).

The user selected PvE design as a development track. No mechanics or implementation slice have yet been approved.

| Stage | Outcome | Prerequisite and closing check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Agree the experience and smallest encounter | Settle first-slice questions; record approved rules, departures, non-goals and acceptance. |
| First playable encounter | Players can start, play and finish the agreed encounter | Approved design and bounded implementation request; focused rules/AI checks, real player journey and affected existing-mode regressions. |
| Playtest and revise | Evaluate the intended experience | Playtest evidence; triage problems and select the next bounded outcome. |
| Expand | Add content/systems justified by the played slice | Separate scope and acceptance; assess shared engine, persistence and multiplayer impact. |

These are provisional planning stages, not authorized milestones. Allocate work IDs when selected; detail the next playable slice instead of the entire mode. Existing bots do not establish PvE completion or select its enemy design.
