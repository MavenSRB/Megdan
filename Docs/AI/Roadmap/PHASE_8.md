### [x] 8.88 One composable target selection before the next hero

Recorded as a proposal 2026-09-23 at the user's request ("Yeah, do it") after a read-only audit, and AUTHORIZED the same day ("go do it"). It does not open a hero.

Why, measured: of the 168 capability identities the build declares, about 30 are target or destination selections, and the ~25 filter parameters on the shared family (`excludeStraightLine`, `excludeAdjacent`, `relation`, adjacency-to-obstacles, ...) each exist on some arms only, so a new phrase mints a capability instead of composing (Ignatia's four Range narrowings in `IgnatiaAttackTargetingContent.cs` say so in their own summary, and mirror `PrintedIconTargetHandler`'s Range arm to do it). Whole blocks went to selection vocabulary (8.38b, 8.39b) and most of 8.57b/8.57d, 8.69a and 8.75's targeting. A survey of the ten undealt heroes' fronts (191) and Gydion's 22 spell cards (Tabletopia extraction s799373; printed-edition wording not re-verified) sorted their targeting phrases as about 43 already expressible, about 43 combinations of concepts the build already has but cannot combine, and about 10 genuinely new predicates. The combinations cluster in Mortimer, Mrak, Trinkets, Gydion, Nebkher and Wuk and follow four shapes: measuring from something other than the acting hero (the Turret, a token, the attack's target, any of Razzle's bodies); tokens of a named kind as the chosen subject; kind unions ("a token, or an enemy unit"); adjacency to a kind with a count ("adjacent to two or more Zombie tokens", "adjacent to terrain, or to a rock token"). Nebkher's Crack in Reality bars interaction across a line for every selection at once, which is one filter over a shared selection and a change to every handler otherwise.

Outcome: one registered selection capability whose candidate kind (hero, minion, unit, obstacle, token of a named kind, space, and unions of them), origin (the acting hero, the enclosing Attack's target, a published output, a token of a named kind), geometry (adjacent, printed Range or Radius, maximum, straight line or not, not adjacent), relation, adjacency-to-kind with a minimum count, exclusions (already chosen, a different target) and count are independent declared parameters, each legal on every arm; placement destinations compose the same filters. It carries the feasibility, anticipated-outcome and consumer-narrowing contracts once, reads Range and Radius through the existing value queries and immunity through `TargetQuery`, and re-derives on answer and restore. Every existing selection identity stays registered and delegates to it, so no card definition, content digest, snapshot schema or wire protocol moves.

Acceptance: behaviour-preservation first - the complete Edit Mode and Play Mode suites and the harness green with zero new skips and every existing gate table unchanged, the configured content digest re-measured unchanged, schema v44 and wire `/21` unmoved. Then expressiveness: focused Edit Mode fixtures that declare, as definition data with no new handler, synthetic nodes for each of the four shapes above drawn from the survey (at least Mortimer's hero-in-radius-adjacent-to-a-token, Mrak's hero-in-radius-adjacent-to-terrain-or-a-token-kind, Trinkets' unit-in-range-of-both-you-and-a-token, and a token-or-enemy-unit union), each with positive, negative, boundary, immunity and restore arms. A conformance test that every filter parameter is accepted on every kind and geometry arm or refused at content load by name. T3.

Non-goals: dealing or composing any undealt hero; the ten new predicates (they arrive with their heroes as filters); re-pointing existing card definitions at the new identity; auras, presence counts and trigger conditions (a later milestone if the dealings show the need); any rule reinterpretation.

Amended during implementation (2026-09-23), within the approved outcome:
