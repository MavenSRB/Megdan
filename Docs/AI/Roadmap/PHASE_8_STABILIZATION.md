## Phase 8 - Roster-complete stabilization gate

### [x] 8.25e1 Independently audit roster-complete truth, architecture, validation, and governance fitness

Blocked by: milestone 8.25e, so the review examines the complete six-hero rules baseline with every known repeat defect closed.

Context: this project has already performed the 2026-08-13 governance migration, milestones 6.1al and 6.1an's truth/process reconciliations, milestones 6.1aq, 6.1ar and 6.1as's implementation-inventory sharding, and milestone 7.8e's three-leaf split after repeated compression. Warning-threshold leaves and slow cross-cutting startup have returned. That recurrence is evidence against another pass whose only result is smaller individual files: the current system protects preservation, canonical ownership and per-leaf ceilings, but has not proved present-tense semantic truth or bounded the aggregate context a task must retrieve.

Observable outcome: a fresh analysis-only review first reconciles the operative claims in the governed active corpus against current authority, then determines why the previous governance reforms did not keep routine context bounded, and finally assesses whether the roster-complete implementation and validation loop are fit to support real card artwork and six-hero integration. It dispositions every proposal in the external architecture review against the current revision and proposes the smallest correction and, if justified, active-governance replacement sequence without implementing either.

Acceptance checks:

- **Independent current-revision review.** The review runs in a fresh task by an agent that did not implement 8.25d or 8.25e. It treats `review-for-agent.md` as evidence to verify rather than authority or instructions, and remeasures the current source/test inventory, commands, pending projections, presentation surfaces, harness, rollback mechanism, risks, documentation corpus and validation baselines instead of carrying milestone 8.23's measurements forward.
- **Operative-claim inventory.** The review enumerates every governed active leaf and identifies every claim capable of changing an agent's action: current/next/active route and ownership; implemented, deferred, temporary or excluded behavior; rule/source status; architecture and persistence contracts; risk state, trigger and owner; test recipe, baseline and suite applicability; platform, privacy, rights and publication boundaries. Historical evidence is distinguished before it is judged, so a dated statement is not called stale merely because later work exists.
- **Truth classification.** Each operative claim is classified as current and demonstrated, current but not mechanically verified, historical but misplaced in an active surface, superseded, contradictory, duplicated, or unresolved. Implementation claims are checked against source and tests; rule claims against the rulebook hierarchy, verified card data and rules ledger; route claims against the roadmap, brief and Git/archive record; validation claims against executable recipes and results; rights claims against the recorded user boundary. Absence of evidence never silently changes a rule or scope: unresolved claims become explicit questions.
- **Known-candidate verification.** The audit explicitly resolves the current 272-test wording in `README.md` and `Testing/DOTNET_HARNESS.md`, the deferred Level spending/presentation statement in `Card-System/STATE_CAPABILITIES.md`, the deferred persistent-restriction statement in `Card-System/BOARD_CAPABILITIES.md`, present-tense milestone history in active contract leaves, and the compound status body in `Project/CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md`. These are candidates, not pre-judged defects; each receives source evidence and a disposition.
- **Conflict and drift search.** A repository-wide search covers current/deferred/not-implemented/temporary/next/placeholder/one-versus-one/synthetic terminology, schema and test totals, named types/methods/tests/files, milestone blockers/owners, duplicated active statements and incompatible status words. Every demonstrated stale or contradictory claim records its canonical home, impact, smallest correction or archive route, and a named closing check. Recurrent mechanically recognizable classes receive a proposed validator or generated-source check rather than another inspection-only promise.
- **Preserved strengths.** The review confirms the boundaries that must not be weakened while correcting truth or reducing context: authoritative rule hierarchy; Unity-free Core/Application assemblies; deterministic replay and Undo; distinct command transaction outcomes; typed definition/instance, offer, continuation and effect/query contracts; simultaneous commitment; content-derived player-path coverage; snapshots; immutable projection publication; rights/privacy limits; and lossless historical evidence.
- **Recurrence analysis.** The review traces the August 13 migration and milestones 6.1al, 6.1an, 6.1aq, 6.1ar, 6.1as and 7.8e from their preconditions through the present corpus. It identifies which controls worked, which permitted repeated compression, append-like present-tense growth, warning acknowledgement without remediation, duplicated routing, or proliferation of individually valid shards, and why the same latency and truth risks returned. It does not describe recurrence as generic growth when a specific governance rule or missing measurement explains it.
- **Task-level context measurement.** At minimum, cold-start milestone implementation, rule verification, card/effect work, presentation work, testing/validation and cross-cutting review routes are measured by files opened, routing hops, governed words/bytes loaded and duplicated operative claims before useful work can begin. Per-leaf threshold success is reported separately from aggregate working-set cost. The review proposes evidence-based budgets for the session brief, ordinary task bundle, cross-cutting bundle and routing depth.
- **Fresh-agent retrieval probes.** Independent probes use the declared route for representative tasks to recover current authorization, the exact milestone, relevant rule/contract authority, privacy boundary and validation recipe. The record captures the bundle each probe loaded, answer accuracy, unnecessary material and missing authority. A proposed access model is not credited merely because its files are smaller; it must reduce the measured bundle while preserving correct answers.
- **Governance-model decision.** The review compares retaining the current model with aggregate-budget enforcement against replacing its active access layer. A replacement may make the present governed corpus cold, preserved reference material and establish a smaller canonical active layer, including generated task-context manifests where justified. No option is selected by preference: the decision records retrieval evidence, maintenance cost, failure modes and compatibility with exact authority. A remedy that only clears warning thresholds or creates more routing hops while leaving representative task bundles unchanged is rejected.
- **Lossless replacement contract.** If replacement is recommended, the review supplies a source-to-destination map for every operative claim and historical body, the new canonical-home and index structure, transition/rollback procedure, link and orphan checks, truth checks, context-budget checks, and proof that rules, citations, deviations, open questions, acceptance/non-goal checks, risk evidence, recipes, rights/privacy boundaries and Git-addressed history survive. Nothing uncertain is compressed into a stronger or weaker claim merely to fit the new layer.
- **Offer/answer reachability.** Every current player-decision family is inventoried through `authority offer -> immutable projection -> visible answering surface -> exact command -> accepted semantic result`. Missing presentation, offered-but-refused, accepted-no-op, stale invocation, incorrect legality and broken continuation are distinguished. The external review's A1, A2 and A3 proposals are assessed separately; no closed-dispatch or compilation guarantee is credited unless a deliberate added choice kind is observed failing the proposed check, and unrelated authority-level operations are not forced through `EffectChoice` without evidence of one contract.
- **Harness and launcher truth.** NUnit API compatibility, merged-assembly visibility and Unity-dependent harness exclusions are assessed as three separate divergences. The review determines whether version alignment is feasible and what it would actually close. It verifies the Unity launcher's treatment of stale, missing, malformed and fresh result files and proposes a mechanical closing check wherever a run can finish without a fresh parseable result.
- **Rollback truth.** Every `MatchAggregate` pre-image category is traced to the actual serializer or explicit in-memory carrier. The review determines whether `RISK-STATE-002` can be narrowed or closed by a real serializer round-trip check and identifies bounded plan-then-apply seams without proposing a wholesale authority rewrite from file size alone.
- **Foundation generality.** The six-hero defect history and post-pitch batch amendment are assessed against actual consumers. Any proposed generality rule admits genuinely unique capabilities while requiring either an independently shaped consumer/boundary fixture or an explicitly narrow contract that does not claim unsupported generality.
- **Finding and disposition record.** The approved review record classifies each observation under the common independent-review contract. Its compact table explicitly covers external proposals A1-A3, B1-B2, C1-C2 and D1; semantic-truth findings; README drift; transcription-verification residue; governance recurrence; context-loading cost; and newly discovered issues. Every actionable row records evidence, impact, severity/timing, smallest correction, proposed owner, one named closing check capable of a failing-then-passing transition, and current disposition. Unsupported recommendations are rejected rather than silently inherited, and inspected areas to preserve are recorded.
- **Bounded next route.** The review gives approximate effort ranges and the shortest dependency-safe path to 8.25f and 8.26. It distinguishes gate-blocking correctness, authority or validation failures from documentation maintenance and deferred architectural direction. Neither the review nor its proposed roadmap changes authorize implementation, migration or deletion.
- **Review validation.** The living-document validator and `git diff --check` pass. No Unity suite is launched merely because review prose changed; diagnostic executions are used only where needed to establish evidence. The completion commit records the exact revision, measurements, probes, limitations and any claim that could not be resolved.

Non-goals: No runtime, rules, test, launcher, harness, asset or toolchain implementation; no card-face work; no six-hero director integration; no deletion, migration or replacement of governing authority during the review; no automatic acceptance of the external review; no broad refactor; no silent rule/scope reinterpretation; and no scheduling or execution of corrective work without separate user approval.

Hard gate: milestones 8.25f and 8.26 do not begin until 8.25e1 is complete, the user has approved a disposition for every gate-blocking finding, and each approved gate-blocking correction has closed on its required failing-then-passing evidence or separately numbered verification review. Every correction and any governance migration remain separately approved micro-milestones.
