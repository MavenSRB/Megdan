## Administrative gates

S1/S3/S4: submission deadline **16 November 2026 at noon**; portal registration deadline **13 November 2026**. Plan earlier: registration processing may take three business days. Application is in English, except the explicitly supplied Serbian declaration. Use current protected templates, fill all substantive fields, preserve structure, export the application directly to PDF and upload both required PDFs. Do not unlock/rewrite template labels or formulas. Upload alone is not submission: the portal's Submit action is required, with explicit user authorization.

The English manual's section 3.2 contains a residual “version 5” reference despite its 6.0 title and current 6.0 downloads. Record and clarify, do not switch to an old form. The public call's “original format” wording concerns preserving the official template; manual and application instructions explicitly require PDF delivery.

The English video is at most three minutes, includes the problem, idea, target fit and every team member's role/contribution, with all members present and visible. A slide-only synthetic-voice video is not acceptable. Keep the link accessible throughout evaluation; post-deadline video replacements are not considered. This is distinct from the approximately 15-minute final implementation presentation.

Limited basic administrative corrections may be requested within 72 hours; incomplete substantive answers, biographies or eligibility cannot be repaired on that basis. Do not plan around this exception.

## Applicant eligibility — unverified until supplied

S3 section 2.2 and S5: 2–5 people; at least 51% Serbian residents and at least 51% without shares in another Serbian company. Each person can participate in only one current-call application across Fund programs; active Fund projects, including external collaboration, require checking. Prior substantially similar evaluated applications must be disclosed; the limit is three submissions across programs.

If awarded, establish the required Serbian LLC before contracting. At least 51% of proposed team members must together own 100%: both members for a two-person team, at least two of three, or at least three of four/five. Founders must meet residency, other-company-share and other-company-representative restrictions; the new company's representative must be a founder. Relevant restrictions extend through evaluation and implementation. S7 distinguishes an entrepreneur from holding company shares: do not automatically reject a team member merely for having an entrepreneurial business.

Before contracting, S3 lists APR company/founder registration, Register of Bidders, National Innovation System register, dedicated bank account, de minimis declaration and mentor selection, plus any requested documents. Do not create a company or register anything on the user's behalf without authorization.

S9 article 7 describes cumulative de minimis aid up to EUR 300,000 equivalent over the current and preceding two fiscal years; article 12 makes current applicable law controlling. Have an accountant/Fund verify the actual beneficiary, related-entity treatment, prior aid and applicable calculation before signing. Do not infer eligibility from a new company's age alone.

## Evaluation and honest fit

| Criterion | Maximum | Application argument | Missing proof |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Usefulness and competitive advantage | 30 | Deliberate individual-hero team tactics for players who value MOBA coordination without continuous reflex execution | Direct comparison and target-user preference; a reskin alone is explicitly weak under S3 |
| Market potential | 30 | Globally distributable original PC game with a narrow initial audience and testable commercial route | Reachable market size, price acceptance, group recruitment and sustainable support costs |
| Team competence | 40 | Existing implementation and playtest history can substantiate execution | Actual complementary members, roles, achievements, availability and commercial capability |

S3 section 4: 75 points qualifies for the final evaluation round, not a guaranteed award. Final selection considers the same criteria and available funds; the Fund can adjust budgets/conditions. No self-awarded score is evidence of competitiveness. An existing prototype is not, by itself, an explicit exclusion in the reviewed eligibility list; disclose the actual stage and clarify uncertainty instead of pretending the work has not happened.

S6 explicitly identifies video games and interactive media among creative-industry priorities (printed p. 92). It discusses small studios and engineering/art capabilities (p. 93), ICT infrastructure/custom software links (table 9, p. 101), IP-oriented creative-industry development (p. 106), and close creative-industry/software connections (p. 107). Our interpretation: an original game developed in Serbia for an international audience is a credible thematic fit. This does not prove applicant eligibility or market demand. Historical statistics in this strategy must not be presented as current market sizing.

Platform context is not the same as product demand. Valve's [Steam Year in Review 2025](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/steamworks/announcements/detail/528746884222682053) reports 42 million peak concurrent platform users in 2025. This supports global PC distribution as technically plausible, but it is not Megdan's TAM, reachable audience or sales forecast. Steam's [official Direct Fee documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee) records a USD 100 per-product fee, recoupable only after USD 1,000 adjusted gross revenue; actual platform deductions, taxes, refunds, regional pricing and operating costs still require a project-specific financial model.

Do not claim “no game like this has ever existed,” an empty market or proven commercial demand. Broader real-time MOBA, tactical and tabletop substitutes remain to be compared with dated primary sources.

## Rights and delivery obligations

S5 requires truthful declarations about rights in the technology and final product, third-party claims, criminal/conflict conditions and other Fund engagements. A future intention to replace borrowed content does not justify signing a false present-tense rights statement. Prepare a provenance register covering code, dependencies, card text, maps, artwork, audio, fonts, branding and contributor assignments. AI tool permission is not by itself an assurance of exclusive copyright or non-infringement; record terms, references and human work and obtain appropriate review.

S3 requires newly created IP/know-how to belong to the beneficiary, secured in third-party contracts. Reports require mentor approval and Fund approval for payments. Keep digital and physical project records for at least five years; allow audit/monitoring, acknowledge Fund support, report changes, avoid double funding and comply with approved costs. Breach can require repayment. S10 says routine ESQ is not required for Smart Start but the Fund may request assessment when concerns arise.
