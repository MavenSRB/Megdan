S7 questions 15–16 and 29: the applicant supplies at least 10% in cash, independently of the Fund and phase by phase. Permitted source families expressly include existing/potential own, other private or venture capital, private-sector debt financing and other private cash contributions. Before each Fund payment, the beneficiary provides a bank statement showing the applicable contribution deposited to the dedicated project account. Applicant fact: Stefan is the sole planned source of the RSD 600,000 contribution; Veljko is not a cash source.

The best-supported provisional company-law vehicle is a **member's additional cash payment** (`dodatna uplata člana`) rather than a grant-funded repayment or interest-bearing project cost. [Company Law articles 178–180 and 182](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/skupstina/zakon/2011/36/1/reg), reviewed 23 October 2026, allow the founding act or a shareholders' decision to set a different proportion or exact amount, so Stefan alone can pay RSD 600,000 without changing the 70/30 ownership or registered capital. The payment must be cash. Repayment is not automatic: it is subordinate to loss/creditor protection and company-law payment limits; [APR guidance](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registri/privredna-drustva/uputstva/promene-.2031.html) requires publication of the repayment decision and a three-month creditor-protection period. Put the exact Stefan-only obligation and any repayment timing in the founding act/decision, deposit the phase amount to the dedicated project account, retain the decision and bank trail, and do not repay it during the project or from Fund proceeds. The FAQ does not expressly name this instrument, so the final financing agreement/Fund and accountant must confirm its Smart Start evidence treatment. A founder loan remains legally possible but is not the preferred dossier route; interest and debt-service costs are ineligible.

Steam [bundles](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/bundles) combine different products/packages and do not solve three copies of the same game. Therefore the team must confirm a compliant external-key/direct-sales route or replace the three-player offer with a platform-supported promotion before promising it. Valve's [pricing guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/pricing) also recommends considering replayability, multiplayer and comparable products and testing market value rather than merely undercutting alternatives.

Do not claim “no game like this has ever existed,” an empty market or proven commercial demand. The focused comparison above covers relevant substitutes; commercial sizing and preference evidence remain open.

## Rights and delivery obligations

S5 requires truthful declarations about rights in the technology and final product, third-party claims, criminal/conflict conditions and other Fund engagements. A future intention to replace borrowed content does not justify signing a false present-tense rights statement. Prepare a provenance register covering code, dependencies, card text, maps, artwork, audio, fonts, branding and contributor assignments. AI tool permission is not by itself an assurance of exclusive copyright or non-infringement; record terms, references and human work and obtain appropriate review.

S3 requires newly created IP/know-how to belong to the beneficiary, secured in third-party contracts. Reports require mentor approval and Fund approval for payments. Keep digital and physical project records for at least five years; allow audit/monitoring, acknowledge Fund support, report changes, avoid double funding and comply with approved costs. Breach can require repayment. S10 says routine ESQ is not required for Smart Start but the Fund may request assessment when concerns arise.
