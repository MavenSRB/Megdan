## Positioning decision

Primary fit: creative industries, specifically video games and interactive media. Proposed customer value: MOBA-style team coordination and individual hero responsibility through deliberate tactical decisions rather than continuous reflex execution. Target hypothesis: adult current/former MOBA players seeking this experience with friends. Age alone does not establish a need or willingness to pay.

The user confirms AI-assisted development. Describe AI as a production tool, including code assistance and intended synthetic artwork, not as a novel AI technology inside the product. Any claim of faster or cheaper production needs measured task-level comparisons. Do not invent AI features to qualify for another priority domain.

Original mythology-inspired expression supports product identity; changing names/art alone does not establish competitive innovation. The application must explain the distinct player experience and why a reachable audience values it.

## Evidence boundaries

Existing playable builds and match histories are valuable evidence of technical execution and sustained use of the private prototype. They are not evidence that the new original content has already been tested.

The preliminary local review of `Builds/**/match-*.log` found 67 distinct files, including 42 with a start event. A selected subset contained 14 completed, long-duration six-seat matches, approximately 21.1 elapsed match-hours, with mean/median around 90.6 minutes and a 71.2–128.7 minute range. Starts in that subset span 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-08 UTC; the last completion is on October 9 in local time. These are provisional internal figures, not approved application statistics: the filter, session deduplication, host/client roles, smoke-test exclusions and coverage of newer log formats still require a reproducible audit before external use.

Six claimed seats do not prove six distinct humans. Logs alone do not identify age, MOBA background, voluntary return, friend referrals, purchase intention or population retention. Elapsed match time may include pauses. Do not multiply match-hours by six and report verified human player-hours. `SessionComplete` is not a match-end event. Seven wins per side in a small selected subset would not prove balance.

Do not discard existing evidence or restart validation from zero. Audit it, obtain participant context with consent, then add the target-group survey required by the program and tests of the original MVP. Existing long sessions also mean that “short matches for busy adults” is not currently a supported promise.
