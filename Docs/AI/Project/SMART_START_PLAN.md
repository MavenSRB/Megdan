## Validation that builds on existing play

The application tests whether the self-funded prototype can become an original, independently understandable MVP that target players voluntarily return to. The current Fund inquiry is saved and unsent; its wording does not replace required validation and business-model reporting.

Reuse the existing reproducible historical evidence audit; update it only for new evidence or an identified discrepancy. Preserve build/session IDs, source/build distinctions, duplicate/test exclusions and privacy limits. Publish only approved aggregates, never raw private logs or personal details.

Before submission, Stefan will obtain consented structured feedback from existing players on MOBA background, valued features, frustrations and actual return behavior. This is missing evidence, not completed research. During the project, recruit a conservative 12–18 new players outside the existing playtest group, with room to exceed that lower-end target. Use scheduled sessions that accept individuals and friend groups, manually coordinated through current lobbies. The target-group survey remains required; its final sample and instrument follow the research question and accessible cohort, without inventing a separate 50-response commitment. The pilot is not a population estimate.

Measure separately:

- Qualified participant: current/former MOBA experience, preferred play style and available session time; age only with consent and a reason to collect it.
- Activation: invited, installed, joined, completed first match; record each denominator and reasons for dropping out.
- Return: record invitations, feasible opportunities and actual second sessions. Separate unsolicited requests, prompted attendance and repeat participation. An initial working target is at least half of new participants returning when offered workable dates; it is neither a Fund requirement nor proof of demand and must be interpreted with denominators and reasons for non-return.
- Social acquisition: actual invited friend participates, not merely “would invite.”
- Commercial evidence: local Serbian engagement does not establish international willingness to pay; test pricing later with the intended paying audience. Stated price acceptance is weaker than a genuine purchase; no payment collection is authorized. Later commercial tests need separate consent and scope.
- Experience: duration median/spread, pauses, waiting, comprehension, coordination and enjoyment, by cohort/build. Long sessions can be acceptable if the audience wants them; do not change the promise to fit an assumed short-session market.

Start onboarding with a quick-start guide covering cards, round structure and victory, recommending the simplest heroes. Reduce facilitator help gradually and observe independent decisions; add guidance at observed sticking points. Timers remain a group choice. A dedicated tutorial mode is not committed. Before tests, refine decision thresholds with the applicant/mentor for comprehension, completion, return, pricing and group formation. Record negative results and pivots. Do not promise product-market fit merely for delivering the reports.
