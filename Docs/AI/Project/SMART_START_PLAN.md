## Validation that builds on existing play

The agreed short inquiry does not include the phrase about market validation. This editorial choice does not remove the program's survey, business-model or reporting requirements below. Preserve them in application planning without presenting them as already completed.

First, reproduce the historical evidence audit: inventory relevant log formats across builds; record build/session IDs, host/client roles, start/end events, UTC timestamps, elapsed time and completion state; deduplicate mirrored sessions; label smoke tests and developer-controlled seats; retain exclusion reasons. Publish only approved aggregates, never raw private logs or personal details.

Then establish target-user context through consented research. The program requires a relevant target-group survey; it need not replace gameplay evidence. Proposed pilot sizes, not Fund requirements or approved commitments: 15–20 problem interviews, 50 qualified survey responses and an original-MVP cohort of at least 24 people from multiple groups. Check feasibility and agree success thresholds before recruitment; a small convenience sample does not estimate population demand.

Measure separately:

- Qualified participant: current/former MOBA experience, preferred play style and available session time; age only with consent and a reason to collect it.
- Activation: invited, installed, joined, completed first match; record each denominator and reasons for dropping out.
- Return: unique participant completes another session within a predefined window, with opportunity to return; distinguish scheduled tests, compensation and voluntary play.
- Social acquisition: actual invited friend participates, not merely “would invite.”
- Commercial evidence: stated price acceptance is weaker than a genuine purchase; no payment collection is authorized. Later commercial tests need separate consent and scope.
- Experience: duration median/spread, pauses, waiting, comprehension, coordination and enjoyment, by cohort/build. Long sessions can be acceptable if the audience wants them; do not change the promise to fit an assumed short-session market.

Before tests, define decision thresholds with the applicant/mentor for comprehension, completion, return, pricing and group formation. Record negative results and pivots. Do not promise product-market fit merely for delivering the reports.

### Pricing hypothesis

Test USD 19.99 for one license against a three-player value target of USD 44.99 total (USD 15.00 per player, approximately 25% below three individual copies at USD 59.97). Ask unaided willingness-to-pay questions before showing either price. Separate “sounds fair” from a concrete choice, identify whether one organizer or three individuals would pay, and record currency/region. No payment collection is authorized by this plan.

Do not describe the group target as a Steam three-pack: current Steamworks documentation says new packages cannot grant multiple copies of the same application. Confirm a compliant direct/external-key path and its payment, tax, refund, fraud and key-policy consequences, or test a platform-supported alternative. Until then the bottom-up model may compare individual and group economics, but the group channel remains unresolved.

### Post-grant publisher route

The grant outcome should be usable for publisher due diligence: an original rights-cleared six-hero MVP; reproducible player and technical evidence; defined audience and price hypotheses; remaining full-production scope, schedule and budget; operating assumptions; team biographies and capacity; and a concise pitch/video package. During the project, define what kind of publisher is suitable: multiplayer PC experience, relevant territories and channels, production/QA capability, marketing capacity, transparent recoup and revenue terms, IP/control expectations and realistic support for a small Serbian studio.

For avoidance of doubt, this MVP is the completed vertical slice defined in the preparation owner: one production-quality original map and one full 3v3 match with six final slice heroes, final slice card/UI art, all required 3D board elements, camera, animations/VFX, game-state feedback and required music/ambience/UI/gameplay sound. It must install, host/join, play through and finish reliably on the defined target PC configuration, with affected reconnect/resynchronization behavior checked. Voice/barks are not required by the baseline and enter scope only if expressly included after quotation. Content beyond this bounded slice remains the publisher-stage production plan.

Publisher outreach, negotiation and any exclusivity or rights transfer occur only after separate approval; no interested publisher currently exists in the evidence set. A publisher is the preferred scaling route, not a substitute for validating the customer problem or understanding unit economics. If the MVP does not justify outreach or suitable terms are unavailable, the final decision must explicitly choose further validation, reduced-scope self-publishing, other financing or stopping rather than assume launch.
