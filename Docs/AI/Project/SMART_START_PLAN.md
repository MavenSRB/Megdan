# Smart Start — proposed delivery and budget

Return to [preparation owner](SMART_START.md). This is a proposal for discussion, not implementation authorization, a spending commitment or a promise of funding. Source requirements: [register](SMART_START_REQUIREMENTS.md), S3 sections 2.3–2.4 and 5; S8 reporting templates.

## Nine-month proposal

Months run from the eventual contractual start, not today's date. Applicant availability and approved scope must determine final dates.

| Stage | Proposed timing | Deliverable and closing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline and plan | Month 1 | Rights-cleared technical baseline, exact original-MVP scope, named owners, costs, measurement protocol and mentor/Fund-approved milestone plan |
| Problem/market validation | Months 1–3 | Target-group survey, methods/sample/limitations, audited historical evidence and explicit decision on audience/value proposition; validation report |
| Original playable slice | Months 2–5 | Six approved original hero kits/cards, one original map and coherent artwork/terminology; invite/join/play/complete journey; affected rules/replay/network tests and observed sessions |
| MVP validation | Months 5–6 | Original-MVP demonstration, usability and technical evidence, issue disposition, rights manifest; MVP report in the second half of the contract, not earlier |
| Business model | Months 6–8 | Competitor and pricing evidence, acquisition/group-formation assumptions, cost model, financial forecast and launch decision; business-model report |
| Final demonstration | Month 9 | Final report, approximately 15-minute presentation, honest before/after development status, future financing plan and settlement requirements |

The reports occur in the required order even if development activities overlap. Agree exact dates with the mentor/Fund. Standard duration is 6–9 months; exceptional extension up to three months needs justification and Fund approval, requested at least 15 days before the end. Do not rely on an extension to make the original plan feasible.

Not included: redesigning all 32 legacy heroes, ranked matchmaking, a live-service backend, mobile release, extra modes, a large content catalogue or AI gameplay research. Scope changes require a decision, not gradual additions to the grant promise.

## Validation that builds on existing play

First, reproduce the historical evidence audit: inventory relevant log formats across builds; record build/session IDs, host/client roles, start/end events, UTC timestamps, elapsed time and completion state; deduplicate mirrored sessions; label smoke tests and developer-controlled seats; retain exclusion reasons. Publish only approved aggregates, never raw private logs or personal details.

Then establish target-user context through consented research. The program requires a relevant target-group survey; it need not replace gameplay evidence. Proposed pilot sizes, not Fund requirements or approved commitments: 15–20 problem interviews, 50 qualified survey responses and an original-MVP cohort of at least 24 people from multiple groups. Check feasibility and agree success thresholds before recruitment; a small convenience sample does not estimate population demand.

Measure separately:

- Qualified participant: current/former MOBA experience, preferred play style and available session time; age only with consent and a reason to collect it.
- Activation: invited, installed, joined, completed first match; record each denominator and reasons for dropping out.
- Return: unique participant completes another session within a predefined window, with opportunity to return; distinguish scheduled tests, compensation and voluntary play.
- Social acquisition: actual invited friend participates, not merely “would invite.”
- Commercial evidence: stated price acceptance is weaker than a genuine purchase; no payment collection is authorized. Later commercial tests need separate consent and scope.
- Experience: duration median/spread, pauses, waiting, comprehension, coordination and enjoyment, by cohort/build. Long sessions can be acceptable if the audience wants them; do not change the promise to fit an assumed short-session market.

Before tests, define decision thresholds with the applicant/mentor for comprehension, completion, return, pricing and group formation. Record negative results and pivots. Do not promise product-market fit merely for delivering the reports.

## Illustrative budget — replace with quotations and actual people

All figures below are planning assumptions in RSD, excluding VAT; not estimates of confirmed market prices. Nine months, two core contributors are placeholders, not a claim about the actual team.

| Official category | Illustrative calculation | RSD |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Human resources | 2 people × 9 months × 240,000 employer gross monthly cost | 4,320,000 |
| Office/business support | 9 × 40,000 for itemized accounting, legal and project-tool costs | 360,000 |
| Equipment/supplies | New development equipment, itemized need and quotes required | 300,000 |
| External development/business services | Original production, specialist development and testing, eligibility to confirm item by item | 1,020,000 |
| Total project cost, excluding mentorship | Sum of above | 6,000,000 |
| Fund contribution | 90% of project cost | 5,400,000 |
| Applicant cash contribution | 10% of project cost | 600,000 |
| Ring-fenced mentorship | Fund-supported fixed amount | 600,000 |
| Total including mentorship | Project cost plus mentor | 6,600,000 |

S3 allows no more than RSD 450,000 employer-gross monthly compensation per key person. The proposed model is below that cap but does not establish workload or cost eligibility. Mentor funding is separate, gross, for at least ten working days and cannot be moved to other categories. Mentor agreement is required within three weeks after financing agreement/assignment.

Eligible categories do not make every invoice eligible. Separate marketing, VAT/duties/fees, used/refurbished equipment, in-kind contribution, debt interest, cash payments, double-funded expenditure, land/building/renovation costs, penalties, hospitality, per diems, recruitment/relocation and other exclusions. Payroll taxes/contributions included in eligible compensation are distinct from generally excluded taxes. Do not rebadge advertising or community promotion as external R&D. Budget outside-project cash for excluded costs and approval/payment delays. Historical work is evidence, not automatically a reimbursable project cost.

## Cash flow and reporting

At the maximum illustrative award, S3's five grant tranches are RSD 1,620,000; 1,620,000; 1,080,000; 540,000; 540,000. Mentor portions are zero; 180,000; 180,000; 120,000; 120,000. These are conditional milestone disbursements, not monthly payments or guaranteed dates.

Each stage requires the applicable own-contribution evidence. The first requires the mentor agreement and approved milestone plan; later stages require approved validation, MVP, business-model and final deliverables respectively. The final tranche also requires the final settlement agreement. Do not assume the full grant is available upfront. S7 says the own contribution need not already be held at application, but must be paid during implementation as required. Confirm actual funding source and liquidity before committing.

The public reporting templates ask for assumptions, minimum success criteria, results and next actions, not just activity lists. The business report requires the separate financial forecast, and the final report asks for revenue, employment, IP actions and additional funding. Record zero or no activity when true. Public-facing summaries must exclude confidential details. Old template filenames and copied mentor-attestation labels should be clarified with the Fund, not edited unilaterally.
