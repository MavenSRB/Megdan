## Decision basis

The Serbian Intellectual Property Office distinguishes unprotected ideas, procedures and methods from protected original expression, including text, art and software ([official guidance](https://www.zis.gov.rs/en/rights/copyright-and-related-rights/)). That supports retaining functional mechanics in principle; it does not establish that the entire current implementation/content package is cleared. Repainting and renaming alone are not a clearance test. Mechanics do not automatically require permission merely because another game uses them.

KEEP means a retention candidate, subject to the stated evidence. REPLACE means use original or appropriately licensed expression, or exclude the item from submitted material. LAWYER means a specific unresolved legal judgment, not a finding of infringement. Ordinary authorship/licence collection is team work; ambiguous terms or competing ownership claims escalate to counsel.

3. **Team: document the original-content boundary.** New art and redesigned heroes are the chosen route; abstract card concepts do not by themselves create a copyright-permission requirement. Compare actual submitted material with this decision. Escalate only concrete retained-expression, extracted-data or contractual uncertainty; do not commission a blanket review of kits no longer intended for retention.
4. **Team: document the rights chain.** Retain authorship/licence evidence and appropriate contributor/supplier agreements. Seek legal help if ownership is disputed, terms unclear or an agreement requires professional drafting; this is separate from permission to use general gameplay concepts.
5. **Team: check the declaration against the actual project and material.** Support its rights assurances with the evidence above. Use the saved Fund inquiry for unresolved program interpretation and qualified advice for a specific unresolved legal question. A future redesign promise does not clear borrowed material already included in a submission. No blanket lawyer-signoff requirement is established by this checklist.
