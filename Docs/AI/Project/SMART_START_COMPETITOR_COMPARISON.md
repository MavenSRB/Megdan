## What is actually distinctive

**The strongest supported distinction from Atlas is the linked hand, initiative and frontline economy.** In the prototype, a card is a commitment with timing, a possible action and, when eligible, survival value. A defender can survive while losing a valuable later attack. Four turns with a five-card hand make that loss matter beyond the current exchange. At the same time, clearing or positioning minions changes both local attack/defense conditions and the strategic lane outcome. These are demonstrated properties of the inherited prototype, not newly invented rules.

**Commitment does not mean the same thing in both games.** Megdan commits a card before reveal while leaving many legal execution decisions until the actor resolves. The player's plan can therefore respond to earlier initiative actors within the chosen card's constraints. This combines anticipation with adaptation. That is a player-experience inference from G1 and A1, not a measured claim that either approach is deeper or better.

**Pantheon is a serious close comparator.** Its published overlap prevents using 3v3, cards, a base objective or mythology alone as a unique selling point. Its stated projection-targeting and map/item variation provide concrete comparison axes, but do not establish the absence of Megdan-like resource pressure. P2 is enough to stop describing its cards as mere decorative ability icons; it is not enough to claim a complete understanding of their role.

The unknown defense/hand system is the main remaining comparison dependency. We cannot claim that Pantheon lacks cards used for survival, card-based initiative, minion lanes, or reactive choices merely because its short description omits them. Nor can we infer absent players, poor quality, weak demand or commercial failure from review counts. No competitor installation or playtest was performed.

## Proposed innovation argument for the application

Frame the proposal as a **differentiated digital product experience**: individual hero responsibility, a constrained multi-use hand, initiative-based execution and shared lane/minion objectives. Explain the player consequence: preserving a strong card, surviving an attack and helping the team advance can compete with each other. Do not present novelty as repainting the prototype or inventing each mechanic. Demonstrated engineering, original future expression and competitive usefulness are separate claims.

Proposed English wording, subject to the remaining comparator/design checks:

> Megdan's proposed product innovation is a 3v3 digital team-tactics experience linking a limited hero hand, initiative-based action resolution and reactive card defense to shared lane and minion objectives, so surviving an attack can cost a player's next offensive opportunity. The inherited-rule prototype demonstrates this decision structure; the grant would develop original content and validate the experience, with Atlas Reactor and the newly identified Pantheon Tactics treated as comparators rather than claiming that the category has never existed.

This is a product-differentiation hypothesis, not verified global novelty, an eligibility ruling, a patent claim or evidence that the applicant-selected **Advanced Innovation** level is independently established. The final original design must decide which demonstrated principles it retains; this comparison does not approve a rule change or silently require copying the source kits into the funded product.

## Closing evidence and next research route

Before a strong exclusivity claim about Pantheon, verify its full turn: player/hero control; initial hand and drawing; commitment timing; initiative; attack targeting; defense/discard; defeat/respawn; minions/base progression; and in-match versus persistent upgrades. A dated manual or repeatable observed match is stronger than tags or a trailer. Record unknowns honestly until then. Any installation/playtest or contact is separate work, not performed here.

For validation, show the hand/initiative/lane trade-off to target players and test comprehension, valued differences, waiting time and voluntary return. Compare like-for-like sessions; do not call the existing long matches shorter or use competitor marketing duration as measured evidence. Use the existing [validation plan](SMART_START_PLAN.md#validation-that-builds-on-existing-play), not an added production scope.

The application can already explain its specific design thesis, openly name close competitors and credit its board-game origin. Stronger claims that no equivalent experience exists, or that Megdan is demonstrably preferred, remain unsupported by this research.
