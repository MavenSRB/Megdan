## Identity and selection

Hero identity is permanently linked across surfaces: pointing at a plaque gives that hero's board piece the warm-ivory six-corner glow, while pointing at the board piece changes only the plaque's reserved two-unit top edge to ivory. The same channel also accepts a tied-Initiative hero button and survives ordinary rail/view recomposition without submitting a command.

Clicking a plaque routes its projected hero identity through the same local tray-selection boundary as an unused board-token click. With teammate coordination off, a player seat accepts only its admitted home hero, rendering only that hero's tray even when foreign cards are public; an unbound seat renders no fallback tray. The Director retains inspection/pinning of other heroes until the awaited hero changes. Selection changes no authority, command, visibility rule or serialized state. Public rail history remains available.

[9.58](../Roadmap/PHASE_9_EXPERIMENTS_AND_MAPS.md#x-958-implement-and-gate-experimental-teammate-coordination) adds the opt-in [Teammate coordination experiment](EXPERIMENTAL_FEATURES.md#teammate-coordination). An allied token or plaque opens that ally's current hand read-only, with an explicit identity and My Cards return. A separate allied-hand projection grants no card commands. Right-click suggestions use independent white outlines and the same shared tray-portrait builder, with smaller board badges; existing effect, selection and hover channels remain intact. The owning milestone records gate status.

## Shared hover preview

Resting the pointer on an occupied turn slot, on a discard icon, or on a revealed marker reveals that card's whole printed face beside the rail it belongs to - the 2026-08-21 decision. Since 9.36 the face is placed off the rail column's own edge, out from the left rail and in from the right; 8.42b had placed it off the hovered icon's edge, which drew it over the owner's tray and plaque. The hero-profile hover keeps its place beside the portrait. It is drawn by the tray's one artwork-aware complete-face builder, so one card cannot look like two: the exact private front appears when prepared and the generated face appears otherwise. One preview element serves all three, so two faces never stand at once. The reveal is a reading of the model the rail was already rendered from: it submits nothing, adds no focus stop, is dropped whenever the rail is redrawn, and an empty slot or a facedown marker reveals nothing rather than an empty card. Each icon's assistive label names the exact card and where it is in its lifecycle, so the fact is available without a pointer.

The same preview also serves the Ultimate icon and the hero medallion. Hovering the medallion displays the existing private hero-picker profile, resolved by registered content identity rather than the match's possibly mirrored hero instance ID. Its aspect ratio is preserved, width capped at 744 reference units and size/position clamped inside the rail region; geometry changes reposition it. It is pointer-transparent, leaves the board identity channel intact, and disappears on pointer exit or model replacement. Missing private profile art yields no preview, while the portrait's name/team assistive reading remains. No profile resource is generated or added by this interaction.
## Held-marker reading

The second reading is rulebook p.18's markers, drawn on the rail of the hero **holding** one. A marker property affects its holder where a token property affects its controller, and p.18 describes markers as components "usually given to other heroes", so Tigerclaw's Poison lands on the rail of the enemy he named and not on his: a rule lowering three of a hero's values has to be visible to the player it is applied to. The row carries the marker's display name and one signed textual channel chip per property it changes. Item bonuses use printed symbols instead, while both readings retain the sign and full words in hover and assistive text. Every part is the trusted `MarkerDefinition`'s - supply, printed strength, channels - with no hero-name branch and no presentation-owned accounting. `MatchSessionProjection.HeldMarkers` is the register itself rather than a copy; the reading's lifetime is the attachment's, so it appears when the marker is given and is gone when p.13's holder Defeat or p.8's `Remove Tokens, Return Markers` returns it. Most of a match has no row there, the conditional the strip's Ultimate card is drawn under.
