## Card colours, Tiers, items, the Ultimate, and hero Level

Every definition states a typed colour, a Basic/non-Basic/Ultimate classification, either a Tier or rulebook p.15's explicit absence of one, and p.16's Item icon or its absence, through a profile the constructor refuses to default. Each configured hero holds p.6's whole eighteen-card deck: p.15's five distinct colours in hand with the Gold and Silver Basic pair, and the twelve upgrade cards those lead to plus the Tier IV Ultimate left where p.6 puts them and where p.15's upgrade searches, with the setup validator admitting both resting places. An effect definition can reach card state, so a Skill can require another hero to discard a card of their own choosing; a fixture Skill card binds it and no configured card does.

Every hero has an authoritative current Level, initialized to 1 and bounded by p.15's 1-8 table, a dashboard declaring p.16's four item slots as content, and an authoritative set of items. p.8's Level Up step moves all three, and it is mandatory: at every round's end each hero buys as many consecutive levels as p.15's table lets it afford from the coins p.13's Defeat awards, and each purchase either upgrades one chosen lowest-Tier non-Basic card - returning it to the deck, taking one of its two next-Tier cards into the hand, tucking the other under the matching dashboard slot - or, once every non-Basic card is Tier III, unlocks the Ultimate instead and ends that hero's leveling. p.16's Gain an Item then takes every card in the set-aside location, which is every card that leveling itself set aside: p.15's upgrade is that location's only writer, and a conformance rule fails if a second one appears. That whole transaction is one intra-stage system transition, so its one question and its ordering across levels and heroes are maintained in [Card-System/TIMING_AND_TRANSITIONS.md](../Card-System/TIMING_AND_TRANSITIONS.md). p.8's last step then pays one Pity Coin to every hero that did not level up that round, on that page's own reading rather than p.15's reminder. The round records who levelled, empties it when the round closes, and persists it, because the answer is produced one stage before it is read. All of it persists in the current schema, under restore refusals [CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md](CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION.md) states. Every team's remaining Life counters join them there, because p.13's Defeat is the one rule that spends one. One query decides p.16's bonus for all six icons, and every total this build computes consumes it: Initiative, Attack, Defense, Movement, a Ranged Attack's Range, and the Radius a primary Skill may print under p.9, whose inclusive disk `RadiusQuery` derives and which Red Needle's Place binds rather than a literal. The same composition reaches an Ultimate's own printed icon, which is [ledger question 12](../RULES_LEDGER.md#open-source-and-rules-questions)'s approved interpretation rather than sourced text: a +1 Radius item makes Wasp's High Voltage Radius 4. Unlocking one is p.16's placement and nothing else - the card leaves the deck for the left of its owner's dashboard - so the unlock is the card's own position, and one query derives the permanent ability from there and supplies it exactly while that hero is on the board, restoring it on a legal return and yielding to no cancellation. No presenter reads a colour, Tier, Level, dashboard, item or Ultimate, exactly as none reads a coin balance; the HUD reads the Life counters, because p.13 now changes them. All of it is proved over configured content and not only over fixtures: Wasp's six verified upgrade pairs, the six items whose icons cover all of p.16's and fill all four of her slots, the seventh level that unlocks High Voltage, and the ordinary match that then reads the ability off that card's own position.

What those types state, what the validator checks, why a slot is not an icon, what the Ultimate prints and does not, which refusals follow at placement, unlock, purchase and restore, and how the unplayed-card transitions and a stale answer behave are maintained in [Card-System/CORE_MODEL.md](../Card-System/CORE_MODEL.md) and [Card-System/STATE_CAPABILITIES.md](../Card-System/STATE_CAPABILITIES.md), with the Radius disk's own rules in [Card-System/BOARD_CAPABILITIES.md](../Card-System/BOARD_CAPABILITIES.md), which took the item model at 7.2 and the dashboard at 7.2a.
