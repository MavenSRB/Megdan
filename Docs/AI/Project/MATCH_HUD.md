## The panel and its regions

Every surface sits on one runtime UI Toolkit panel. `ConfiguredMatchHudRenderer` owns the session-free drawing surface: code lays out five absolute regions from viewport functions and answers pointer blocking from live bounds, with those rects as fallback.

Unity's text shaper ships ICU data only when the build contains a `PanelSettings` asset; the committed `Assets/Resources/UI/GuardsRuntimePanelSettings.asset` supplies it, `Instantiate`d so per-panel values stay runtime-only, its `m_ICUDataAsset` empty because asset presence carries the data. Layout stays code-owned behind `I*View`.

**The whole panel can be taken away, and exactly one thing does:** the attack spotlight hides every child of the root but its own layer, restoring each verbatim on exit. It and the easing that replaced popping are [PRESENTATION.md](PRESENTATION.md)'s.

The standing HUD follows the approved [7.8a mockup](../References/MILESTONE_7_8A_UI_MOCKUP.html), [8.7a rail mockup](../References/MILESTONE_8_7A_CURRENT_CARD_RAIL_MOCKUP.png), and 8.29a plaque composition. Its 96-unit status strip is a 56-unit identity row over a 40-unit presenter-derived narration bar - centred and width-capped, its drop-down log unrolling beneath it - carrying round/turn, both Life counters and Wave with the same assistive reading. The retired Tie Breaker coin is now a slim `TIEBREAKER` banner, team-coloured, above the HOLDING team's rail column, easing between columns on handover. The WHOLE strip and each hero's own rail - several at once during commitment - share that warm gold shine exactly while `AwaitedInputQuery` says the match waits on the viewer or, at hero scope, on a commitment, the release owner, or an undecided agreement participant; the bar's warm background reads the viewer's own reading, so the two cannot disagree, and its bold headline names the acting hero and activity - own decision in full, elsewhere only the public fact, Initiative demoted to detail. End Turn, rectangular at 264-by-96, stays aligned to the responsive Blue rail. While the viewer's own finished turn waits on its release it pulses the same blood red across a translucent full-face overlay and its outside ring until pressed; 9.89 shortens the unchanged eased cycle from 800 to 650 ms. The transient centre banner (COMMIT A CARD, YOUR TURN, END TURN, MINION BATTLE, only to the player who is up) holds 2.9 s (9.28). Its outlined action line and final-ten countdown use the viewer's side colour: blood red for Red, dark blue for Blue; an unseated Director defaults Red. The clock paints `10` through `1` at screen centre with no plate or background; its host ignores picking so input passes through. Simultaneous allowances keep the latest-expiry shared header; mixed clocks warn on the earliest final-ten deadline.

[Hero-rail layout](HERO_RAIL_INTERACTION.md#layout) owns full and compact trays, hover expansion, team order and the Director drawer.

The right rail is the left one mirrored rather than repeated, so both read outward from the board and each hero's marker sits on its rail's inner corner. Two rows stay unmirrored: the four resolved-turn slots (a sequence, not decoration) and discard (a pile reads left to right regardless of edge). Which team a hero is on is not written on the rail; the two edges, the accent colours and the hero disc's own hover and assistive reading carry it, as a card's printed colour is carried. [Hero-rail interactions](HERO_RAIL_INTERACTION.md) owns linked identity highlights, plaque selection and portrait pick-card previews.

Escape invokes the current card-inspection or spellbook CLOSE action, one surface per keypress, regardless of whether focus remains in the surface or returns to the HUD. The pre-match Host and Join menus use their existing CANCEL action on Escape; the landing has none. These dismissals never issue a match command.

The W-011 countdown uses 260-unit digits, larger than the 150-unit transient-banner line while retaining its colour and outline.

## The card in play, and the four turns behind it

The rail separates the card a hero has in play now from the turns behind them. A marker in the header's inner corner holds the current card while it is committed or revealed and unresolved; the four numbered slots are resolved turn history, and a turn is numbered only once that turn's exact card instance reaches `CardResolutionStatus.Resolved`.

Both halves are one question asked of the card's own resolution status, and nothing here remembers an answer. The tray asks the same question: a turn resolves hero by hero in Initiative order, so a real position exists with one hero's card resolved and another's not, and milestone 8.5b's "card in play" reading had to be qualified by the same status or the two surfaces would have disagreed about one card. That card's lifecycle line reads `RESOLVING` wherever it is drawn.

A facedown commitment is a neutral card back and `FACEDOWN`, carrying no card rather than a card drawn differently. The projection refuses to name a committed instance until joint reveal, so the marker is built from commitment status alone and cannot leak the card. A revealed marker carries the printed colour and card-plus-item Initiative, calculated by the shared item query from that owner's public counts (8.42a), with no caption. The 2026-08-21 `RESOLVING` reading stays in its hover and generated fallback lifecycle band. Its label - `6`, or `6+1` with an item since 8.55 (the printed Initiative and what the item adds, carried as two numbers) - fills the fixed marker, the type stepping down from 20 to 18 or 14 units for three or four characters, centred on both axes without changing the badge. The printed front stays unchanged, with no synthetic lifecycle strip. The marker's footprint is reserved even when empty, so the header does not move four times a round.

The shared, artwork-aware [hover preview](HERO_RAIL_INTERACTION.md#shared-hover-preview) reveals occupied turn slots, discard icons and revealed markers; empty slots and facedown markers reveal nothing.

One card a hero owns is neither in the round nor in the pile: a card standing in rulebook p.16's Ultimate area draws a small mini card in its hero's item strip after the item chips (9.30a), revealing the same full printed face every other rail icon reveals and reading `UNLOCKED` in its assistive label and, on the generated fallback, its lifecycle band - `TrayCardGroup`'s fifth member, produced only here, because every other lifecycle word claims a turn, hand, pile or deck the unlocked card is not in. It reads the card's own projected location - the unlock *is* the position, by milestone 7.3's modelling - so it appears when p.15's seventh level turns the card faceup, and most of a match draws none. Beside a spellbook owner's card, the team's Wish count reads `n/3` (9.30g, `IMatchReadModel.TeamVictoryTallies`).

A spellbook's owner (9.30h) also draws a `SPELLBOOK n | m` chip (in the book | removed), last of the item chips, opening a non-commanding drawer between the rails, above the hand, scrolling full-size faces (`CLOSE`): book spells the seat's projection names are faces - only its own book - and those it only counts are tier-back stacks with counts (`SeatHandSummary.SpellbookBacks`, basic spells on Tier I); removed spells are faces for all, each revealing its full face under the pointer.

The discard row is a third revealable surface with no number of its own: one compact card-shaped icon per card in its printed colour, carrying no text - sequence is the row's fixed left-to-right positions, the count is the icons, everything else one pointer rest away at full size. If a discarded card still sources an unended Active effect, its miniature keeps the same violet outline as the other standing-source surfaces until that final instance ends; ordinary discards retain the black perimeter. An unoccupied resolved-turn slot stays an empty outline that reveals nothing and answers no pointer: an unfinished turn is not a card. The pile has no authoritative order and none is invented from watched discard events: icons are ordered by card instance identity, as the tray's discarded group is, so one card sits in one place on both surfaces.

The rail carries two readings that belong to the hero rather than to any card. The coin balance is `HeroProgressionProjection.Coins` read straight through - the field p.13's reward, p.8's Pity Coin, card grants and p.15's Level Up move - so it follows every authoritative change with no new projected field. It sits in the item strip, which keeps the numbers true of a hero now; no `RESOLVED TURNS` caption remains. It is a 24-unit struck disc with a centred 13-point amount, not `COINS` beside a number - composed from the hero disc's filled circle, darker rim and centred label, never an imported sprite - with `3 coins` in its hover. It is public and drawn whether or not a coin decision stands: a balance seen only while spending it arrives too late to plan with. Each of the four slots names itself in its hover and assistive label (`Turn 1: Blue card - Static Barrier`, `Turn 3: empty`), as 8.7a moved the team label onto the hero disc.

Held markers appear on the holder's rail, not necessarily their creator's; the [hero-rail marker reading](HERO_RAIL_INTERACTION.md#held-marker-reading) owns its typed source, channels and lifetime.

The plaque prefers 264 reference units, enough for the medallion, full written name and reserved current-card marker to read as one identity, clamped to a quarter of a narrow panel minus its outside margin so two plaques stay strictly below half the viewport; that room also stops secondary rows buying narrowness back as wrapped height. Each edge column stacks p.6's three plaques below the status strip and clear of the hand; the frame clips no readable descendant, and live geometry checks keep every descendant inside its plaque and column. The `DIRECTOR` toggle sits immediately below the third Red plaque, and `CONFIRM FACEDOWN` has its own 300-by-44 lane 18 units below it, drawn only while a card is selected and once real geometry exists (no origin flash), part of neither the contextual dock nor End Turn's corner. Printed Primary and Secondary actions form no duplicate row there; their typed regions sit on the resolving card. The event log and director drawer remain the only expanded surfaces in that lane. Every panel above refreshes independently: one whose rendering throws is reported by name and leaves every other panel - board, tray, End Turn and the seat UNDO included - holding the current projection, where a straight-line refresh skipped every panel behind it and presented a frozen client. Non-item textual channel tokens are three letters (Initiative became `INI` at 8.23a); plaque item bonuses use 8.30k's shared typed printed-symbol catalog.

A resolved slot whose card still sources an unended Active effect wears the violet outline milestone 8.5b gives that card in the tray. The standing-source projection includes dormant and active instances, so a Next Turn promise lights when it is created, remains uninterrupted through its later activation, and unmarks only when its final instance ends. The outline changes colour without changing geometry; assistive wording carries the state without reserving a separate visible label strip.

The board artwork now uses the complete screen behind the HUD. The selected map's complete playable-hex inventory, rather than the square artwork or decorative terrain inventory, is fitted into one exact L-shaped safe surface: the broad arm between and above standing HUD, plus the lower-right dead space beside the compact hand. Every complete playable hex retains a visible moat from cards, rails and the status dock; artwork and non-playable decoration may pass behind those surfaces. [CONFIGURED_PRESENTATION.md](CONFIGURED_PRESENTATION.md) owns the fit itself.

## The card tray

The tray shows one hero's current round. Each card's projected `CardColor` supplies its colour and assistive name; team, hero, position, display name, art and action type do not. Cards still in hand and the resolving card overlap in the bottom-left footprint through panel x=732. Resolution exclusivity may remove a held card's command, but never dims or moves it; the resolving card stays full-strength and stable-sorted rightmost with its face and printed actions exposed. Genuinely played or discarded non-resolving cards alone occupy a compact vertical fan at the bottom-right margin below Blue's rails, using the same complete footprint at full brightness without a dark veil. Its first four faces expose deterministic top strips and its last face is complete. Hover/focus replaces the addressed face above the HUD at full brightness without moving either fan; the hidden resting anchor returns at full brightness on exit. Printed rules stay on the face. Small text tooltips are suppressed throughout the HUD; full card previews, defense breakdowns and underlying labels remain. The right fan disappears when empty, offers no selection or drag, and has a fixed reservation so lifecycle refreshes move neither rails nor map. Other order remains projection-derived. The Director follows the awaited hero and may pin another through an unused map click or plaque. Player trays stay on their admitted hero and show no pre-binding fallback. Only painted card/controller bounds block board input. A casting card's typed spell column sits above the player's own hand face; inspection, Shift-hover token pips, rail/history, read-only hand previews and level-up candidates place it beside the face. Its landscape faces render at 70% scale without duplicate names, status captions or a `CAN CAST` label; a spell unavailable because it left the book is distinguished only by the shared dimmed face treatment. Preview placement bounds the combined face and column below the header; inspection uses only viewer-admitted cards and public spell availability.

Moving an eligible card 12 reference units starts one pointer-captured drag drawn with the shared full-size complete-card face. The exact source slot becomes invisible without reflow while the card is held and returns on cancellation. Dropping on the existing L-shaped map submits that exact projected commitment once; the coordinate is discarded. A complete owner-projection refresh caused by another player's commitment defers the hand rebuild while the exact dragged card remains selectable, then adopts the latest projection before the drop submits; a missing or newly ineligible card still cancels. Off-map, stale, hidden or replaced gestures cancel. Below-threshold click and keyboard selection still require `CONFIRM FACEDOWN`; another seat's absent card cannot enter the gesture.

Selection, availability, resolving, Active source, dragging and lifecycle treatments neither recolour nor replace it; the unchanged card control remains the only pointer/keyboard surface. The ordinary hand does not scroll, while a short mandatory context may scroll vertically without horizontal travel or card resizing.

## A card whose Active effect is standing

A card sourcing any dormant or active instance takes the violet slot outline and, on generated fallback, the word `ACTIVE`; prepared fronts keep that word in typed hover text. `MatchSessionProjection.StandingActiveEffectSourceCardInstanceIds` derives the unique unended sources from authority, so presentation infers nothing and restore, replay and Undo rebuild the same answer. The mechanically active `ActiveEffectSourceCardInstanceIds` remains separate: dormant promises light no hero/radius target and contribute no active value. The always-reserved slot changes only colour, stays outside the invariant black perimeter, and remains independent of cyan selection and orange confirmation.

The card the current turn is being resolved from is drawn at full strength. It carries no commitment command - the round is past choosing - and it takes no focus stop, but "carries no command" and "is spent" are different facts, and the shared unavailable treatment was saying the second about the first. All hand and right-side unavailable cards remain fully bright, including during resolution and hover inspection; availability still controls commands. Awaited and eligible cards keep their commands, assistive reading, selection treatment and full-strength focus, but draw no gold perimeter and do not join the breathing-shine animation; the standing rail/header cues carry that wait signal.

The [typed value-region contract](CARD_VALUE_OVERLAYS.md#printed-value-ownership) governs icon ownership.

The [printed ink and source-text contract](CARD_VALUE_OVERLAYS.md#printed-ink-and-source-text) governs face text and contrast.

The card's printed colour is not written on its face (2026-08-21): wearing and spelling it says one thing twice in the space the text needs. `ColorName` stays on the view model and in each card's hover and assistive reading, where 7.8a keeps a rail turn slot's colour; nothing here means something by colour alone.

## Online match flow

Milestone 8.35b reuses this HUD after the primitive lobby: it binds the admitted seat's owner projection, removes Director tools from joiners, keeps that hero's hand as home, and clears stale selection on Leave or reattachment. The host may retain the separate drawer; W-062 binds it to the encounter for seated and unseated PvE hosts, while an unseated host's full-information player port remains observe-only. Input or recovery waits remove controls while public state stays readable, and a fresh receipt redraws even at an unchanged gameplay version. If that wait temporarily strips an already-drawn Level Up or Tied Initiative's private detail, its owner retains the reading behind the interaction wall until detail returns or authority advances; non-owners never gain it.
