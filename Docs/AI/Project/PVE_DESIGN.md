# PvE design

Canonical owner for original PvE product and rule decisions. Return to [project index](../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md); delivery stages live in [the PvE plan](../Roadmap/PVE.md).

## Approved direction

Design PvE alongside iteration, testing and repairs to the existing private game. The first slice is a single-player **test** encounter shipped as a separate executable; PVE-D28 extends it with three-player co-op. A merged executable remains later work. Its source is co-designer Vorty's draft (2026-10-10: card-driven enemies with publicly committed four-turn plans, three friendly creeps to defend, staged spawns on the official map), refined with the user in a design interview the same day. The records below are approved rules for that slice only; they never change the existing mode.

## PVE-D24 Enemy types and presentation (Approved, user 2026-10-11)

PvE only. Supersedes PVE-D16's placeholder token presentation: enemies use the actual game's red Melee, Ranged and Heavy token artwork on the map and a scrollable enemy left rail with public plans/cards, status and board highlighting. The rail reuses the hero plaque and its four card slots, filled with all four planned cards at round start; hovering a slot uses the shared full card preview. These are the four turns within a round, not four future rounds. They remain heroes under PVE-D06; artwork does not change their rules.

Melee replaces Raider, keeping its card values and plans, but every Attack tries a Blue creep before a hero; Movement pursues creeps. Heavy replaces Brute with its previous cards and priorities unchanged. Ranged is level 1 with A Aim (Initiative 8, Move 2 toward heroes, stop distance 3), B Shoot (two copies, Initiative 6, Attack 3 Range 3, then Move 1 toward heroes stopping at 3), C Power Shot (Initiative 3, Attack 5 Range 3). Both Ranged Attack cards try heroes then creeps. Purple P Guard has Initiative 1, Defense 2. Plans: `A B C B`, `A C B B`, `A B B C`. This is approved placeholder test content, not settled balance.

Keep PVE-D17's coordinates and ordering. Round 1 alternates Melee/Ranged starting with Melee (four/three); round 2 alternates Heavy/Ranged starting with Heavy (two/two). Closing checks: shipped catalog values, priority order, plan multiplicity, spawn mapping, save/editor round-trip, matching map/rail art and projected standing/waiting/defeated status, hover and Undo/Restart presentation.

## PVE-D25 Two attack cards (Approved, user 2026-10-12)

Supersedes PVE-D24's duplicate Slash/Shoot and their plan sequences. Each shipped enemy hand has exactly two attack-capable orange cards, counting copies: Melee has one Slash and one Heavy Blow; Ranged has one Shoot and one Power Shot; Heavy retains one Smash and one Hurl. Melee and Ranged keep their movement card and fill the fourth slot with X Idle (Initiative 1, no action), following PVE-D07. Their plans become `A B C X`, `A C B X`, `A B X C`. Heavy's plans, defenses and all other values, target priorities and spawns are unchanged. Closing checks: every composed hand and listed plan contains exactly two attack cards, four action slots remain valid, content saves round-trip and target-priority checks remain green.

## PVE-D26 Heavy-only defenses (Approved, user 2026-10-12)

Supersedes the Melee/Ranged defenses retained by PVE-D24/D25. Only Heavy enemies have Defense cards: exactly two, retaining Thick Hide (Defense 3) and Brace (Defense 5), both Initiative 1. Melee and Ranged have no Defense cards. All attack cards, movement, idle slots, plans, target priorities and spawns remain unchanged. Closing checks: shipped and composed hands have defense counts 0/0/2 with Heavy's values preserved; empty defense lists validate and survive save/editor round-trip.

## PVE-D27 Three-hero Blue team (Approved, user 2026-10-12)

Supersedes PVE-D02's playable team-size range: the player chooses exactly three distinct configured heroes for Blue. Keep all five PVE-D17 placement spaces as choices for those three heroes. The launcher caps selection at three and permits starting only with three; its programmatic start applies the same Core-owned validation. Generic encounter composition remains available for small deterministic test fixtures. No enemy, deck, placement-coordinate or existing-mode change. Closing checks: reject zero/one/two/four/five player selections, accept three, cap selection and disable Start when incomplete, compose and place exactly three Blue heroes.

## PVE-D28 Three-player co-op (Approved, user 2026-10-12)

Add Host/Join to the separate PvE executable while retaining solo. A playing host and two joiners each control one distinct Blue hero. Only the host runs enemy/encounter automation and decides enemy ties and shared Blue-team choices; hero-owned actions remain with that hero's player. Reuse private keyed TCP and owner-private projections, with reserved-seat reconnect and terminal host loss, no migration. All three players must be present and ready before starting. Existing decks, waves, three-hero count and five placement options remain unchanged. Acceptance: host and two clients agree on encounter state; unauthorized hero/shared/enemy commands are refused, private cards remain private, public enemy plans remain visible, reconnect restores the same seat, and PvP remains unchanged.

## PVE-D29 Compact enemy presentation (Approved, user 2026-10-12)

Supersedes PVE-D24's full hero plaque and PVE-D16's map plan strip: enemy rows show their official token, four public turn-card slots and, only for Heavy, its two Defense cards. Only enemies currently on the board have rows: defeated enemies disappear, and future waves appear when spawned. No hero name/level/item/discard sections or floating text above map tokens. Spent defenses stay visible but dimmed. Preserve shared full-card hover; token-to-row and row-to-token hover highlight the same enemy in solo and co-op. Identity/status remain available through inspection rather than occupying a permanent text row. No rules, cards, plan timing or ownership change. Closing checks: compact geometry, four planned cards, correct Defense slots/state, row spawn/removal, absence of map labels, public-card previews and linked hover in solo and a joining client.

PvE approval never silently changes competitive play. Ambiguous reused printed rules use the ledger hierarchy. Do not guess unresolved mechanics or prebuild a speculative framework.

## Engineering boundaries

Keep rules/state independent of Unity and presentation. Enemies submit legal authoritative actions; UI and AI do not own resolution. Identify shared versus mode-specific changes before implementation, including pending choices, host/seat information, identities, snapshots, Undo and replay. Approved nondeterminism must be explicit, recorded and replayable. Test changed contracts and affected consumers while preserving existing-mode behavior.
